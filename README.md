# Geospatial Cartography & Mapping Portfolio

### Samson Adeyomoye | GIS & Remote Sensing Analyst | Geospatial Developer

A curated collection of geospatial maps, cartographic visualisations, thematic mapping outputs and spatial data products developed through professional engagements, internships, academic exploration and independent projects.

This repository documents my experience in transforming geospatial datasets into meaningful visual representations that communicate spatial patterns, geographic relationships and environmental information.

---

## About This Portfolio

Over the years, I have worked on a variety of mapping and geospatial visualisation tasks, ranging from conventional thematic cartography to satellite-derived mapping products and spatial analysis.

This portfolio brings together selected maps and cartographic outputs produced across different stages of my professional development, including work undertaken during internships, professional engagements and independent exploration.

The collection reflects my practical experience with GIS software, remote sensing technologies, spatial datasets and cartographic design.

## Technical Competencies

- **GIS & Cartography:** ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Desktop, QGIS
- **Remote Sensing:** Google Earth Engine, ENVI, ERDAS Imagine, PCI Geomatica
- **Geospatial Data:** Shapefiles, GeoJSON, raster datasets, DEM, DSM and satellite imagery
- **Earth Observation:** Landsat, Sentinel, MODIS and other geospatial datasets
- **Spatial Analysis:** Geoprocessing, spatial modelling, classification, change detection and terrain analysis
- **Programming & Web Mapping:** Python, JavaScript, Leaflet, Mapbox GL JS and HTML/CSS

## How to Explore

Maps are organised into directories according to their origin, project category and professional context.

Where available, individual map entries include:
- Final cartographic output.
- Project description and geographic coverage.
- Data sources and methodology.
- Software and tools used.
- Relevant project context and acknowledgements.

Some entries are presented primarily as finished cartographic products rather than complete analytical projects. The repository therefore focuses on showcasing mapping outputs and cartographic experience, rather than implying that every entry contains a reproducible workflow.

## Purpose

This portfolio serves as a visual record of my geospatial experience and continuous development in GIS, remote sensing, environmental intelligence and geospatial technology.

It is intended for professional reference, collaboration, research engagement and showcasing my cartographic capabilities.
